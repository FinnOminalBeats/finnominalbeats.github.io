Finn Ominal is a masked, producer-led virtual artist — a Brooklyn-rooted dancehall storyteller building a cinematic black-and-gold world with human songwriting, human production, and openly disclosed AI-assisted vocals and visuals.

The split mask carries the meaning: war on one side, peace on the other — pain and resilience, the producer behind the curtain and the performer at center. Dancehall at the core; afrobeats and reggaeton in the bloodline.

Produced under Urban MindState with the East Side City family.

Openly virtual. Human at the core.
