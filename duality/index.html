The Album

One World,
Changing State

Duality isn’t a split-screen — it’s one world surviving the night. The sound is dark, bass-forward, midtempo and tightly mastered. Finn stands at the balance point: half obsidian, half gold, scarred but calm.

War is ash, ruined stone and storm pressure. Peace is the same city at dawn, reflected in still water — the thin gold crack through the mask is the transition. Here, peace isn’t innocence or softness; it’s the stillness left after surviving conflict.

Human-written patois lyrics, human production, AI-assisted voice — openly disclosed. The record that opened the vocal era.
