The Story

A Whole Song About
One Bad Decision at the Bar

Dancehall for the moment every night out begins: staring down the bar, unable to pick. All Kind turns the liquor shelf into call-and-response — rum, gin, Henny, tequila each get their moment over a bouncy riddim and a crowd-chant hook built to be shouted back.

Finn Ominal is a masked virtual artist out of Brooklyn: human-written patois lyrics, human production, AI-assisted voice — openly disclosed. This is the first single of the nightlife era, with more landing all year.
